## Visibility profiles

One row per scene, one column per board position u relative to the ARES centre (left = towards the wall start), 200 mm grid from -1200 to +1200 mm (configured target positions [-920.0, -220.0, 480.0, 1180.0] included, marked `'`; `|` = ARES centre). Visible with a collision-free 150 mm approach and clearance `#` >= 30 mm, `+` >= 20 mm, `=` >= 10 mm, `-` >= 5 mm, `!` < 5 mm; `c` every IK-feasible look pose collides, `o` collision-free poses exist but no free approach / board occluded / not detected, `.` no look pose with IK in the allowed family, `x` board position occupied by a stone. Clearance = largest shift of the camera pose (+-x, +-y in the ARES frame) that stays collision-free; it also bounds the ARES stop error the planned look pose tolerates.

## Notes on the simulation (RoboDK 6.0.0.26652, observed in this study)

- Cam2D cameras render only if opened while rendering is on (`RDK.Render(True)`); afterwards snapshots also follow robot moves with rendering off.
- A textured object (board PNG) added after the first camera was opened renders black in every camera: all boards are created first.
- RoboDK's collision check does not report a body completely inside another mesh (camera origin 40 mm inside a stone: only the gripper was reported); bodies crossing a surface are reported. Such poses are still rejected by the render (the camera sees stone faces) and the approach test.
- The modelled lens reaches 53 mm in front of the projection centre; it is hidden during each snapshot (otherwise the camera sees its own lens) and shown again for the collision checks.
