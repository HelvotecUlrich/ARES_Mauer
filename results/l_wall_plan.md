## Request and design
- Samuel 2026-10-05: a small L with the wall (it can be a bit shorter), 4 courses, keep the 8 existing wall boards (placed differently), half stones at the ends and maybe the corners.
- The 53 x 100.5 mm pin pattern is not square: a stone turned by 90 deg never engages the sockets below, so the legs cannot interlock - the corner is a vertical BUTT joint: leg A runs through (its end flush with the body of B's outer face; B's ribs stand 1.69 mm proud), leg B starts at A's inside face - beyond A's ribs ([brick] rib_mm 1.69 mm, CAD) and a corner gap ([wall] corner_gap_mm 1 mm, ASSUMPTION): set B's base blocks that far off A's. Half stones at both ends of every odd course make each leg a rectangle (no notch at the corner, no stone supported across legs).
- Wall frame = leg A frame; ARES works from the OUTSIDE of the L on both legs (wall distance 740 mm to each leg centreline); leg A is built completely, then leg B.

## ARES routes

Checked with the chassis 1271 mm diagonal ([ares] length x width), clearance 50 mm to legs, plates, blocks and the station table (rotation: swept circle r 635 mm); every move between stops is a route too (same leg: back off, along the leg, approach - the plates end 10 mm before the ARES front at a stop, so the stop line itself keeps no clearance).
